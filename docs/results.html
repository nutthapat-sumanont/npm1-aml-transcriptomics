3. Replication in TCGA-LAML

Same cohort filters, a different hospital, sequencing centre, genome build and annotation: 111 patients (31 NPM1-mutated, 80 wild-type), design ~ sex + NPM1_status. Every positive control passes again, and a score built only from the Beat AML top 50 up and top 50 down genes separates NPM1-mutated from wild-type TCGA samples with ROC AUC 0.992 (95% CI 0.981 to 1.000).

4. How much is cell maturity?

5. A known subgroup inside NPM1-mutated AML

About 40% of NPM1-mutated samples (37 of 100) have high NEK10, FTO and USP44. This group matches two published NPM1 subgroups: the "primitive" subtype of Mer et al. (Nat Commun 2021) and the IFEX9-high group of Arza-Apalategi et al. (Leukemia 2023). It is not a new finding. 32 of the 37 high samples look primitive by the Mer et al. markers, and the high group carries more TET2 (33% vs 11%) and IDH2 (39% vs 13%) mutations, while the low group carries all the NRAS and PTPN11 mutations.

7. Ex vivo drug response

Beat AML tested each patient's leukaemia cells against a drug panel; lower AUC means fewer cells survive, so more sensitive. Matched to the same specimen as the RNA-seq (206 samples in the analysis set). Across 123 drugs, no drug differs by NPM1 status at FDR < 0.05 after adjusting for the monocyte-like score. No menin inhibitor is in the Beat AML panel, so the menin link stays at the level of expression.

8. Survival (exploratory)

Treatment varied a lot across Beat AML patients and isn't in any model, so these results are exploratory. 275 patients with follow-up, 133 deaths, median follow-up 35 months. Inside NPM1-mutated AML, the NEK10/FTO/USP44-high group's hazard ratio after adjusting for age and FLT3-ITD is 1.19 (95% CI 0.64 to 2.21): the same direction as Mer et al., but with 44 deaths the data can't tell a real effect from no effect.
