# Figure legends (draft, Session 14)

Every number below is taken from LEARNING.md. The figures are built by `scripts/14_paper_figures.R` from tables in `results/`, and the script stops if a number printed on a figure doesn't match LEARNING.md. One number was corrected in Session 14: the primitive score vs core score correlation is 0.90 (0.9048 in `results/subgroup_primitive_committed_scores.csv`), not the 0.91 written in Sessions 5 and 8.

Abbreviations: VST, variance-stabilizing transformation; log2FC, log2 fold change; padj, Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted p-value; NES, normalized enrichment score; AUC, area under the curve; HR, hazard ratio; CI, confidence interval.

## Figure 1. NPM1-mutated AML carries a HOX-centred expression signature that is shared with KMT2A-rearranged AML

Beat AML 2.0, de novo AML at diagnosis, one sample per patient, no APL or KMT2A fusions.

**(A)** Principal component analysis of the 277 samples before QC (VST, top 500 most variable genes). PC1 explains 19.7% and PC2 13.0% of the variance. NPM1 status explains 43% of PC2; wave and specimen type explain less than 5% of any component. Open circle: BA2294R, recorded female but male by expression (XIST off, RPS4Y1 on), excluded after QC as a possible sample swap. The main cohort is 276 patients (100 NPM1-mutated, 176 wild-type).

**(B)** Differential expression, NPM1-mutated vs wild-type (DESeq2, design ~ wave + specimen type + sex + NPM1 status; apeglm-shrunken log2FC). 1,019 genes pass padj < 0.05 and abs(log2FC) > 1: 307 up and 712 down. Labelled: the positive controls HOXA9 (+1.60), HOXA10 (+1.51), MEIS1 (+1.60), PBX3 (+1.91) and CD34 (-4.00); NEK10, the most significant gene; and the FTO locus, whose gene-level count is mostly the IFEX9 transcript (Figure 3D). padj is floored at 1e-300 for plotting.

**(C)** Preranked gene-set enrichment (fgsea) on the DESeq2 Wald statistic, after removing the 25 male-specific chrY genes and XIST (19,372 genes). The Verhaak et al. 2005 NPM1 signature is reproduced: UP NES +3.31 (FDR 2.4e-36), DN NES -3.76 (FDR 1.4e-96). GO Biological Process: the five main terms with the highest and the five with the lowest NES after collapsing redundant terms (468 terms at FDR < 0.05, 154 after collapsing). The HOX terms (embryonic skeletal system morphogenesis, NES +2.57) and a group of cilium terms are up; MHC class II antigen presentation and antigen receptor signalling are down. FDR is corrected within each collection.

**(D)** HOXA9 and MEIS1 expression (blind VST) in NPM1-mutated (n = 100), KMT2A-rearranged (n = 24) and other AML (n = 176). Median HOXA9 12.2, 12.3 and 9.5; median MEIS1 12.2, 12.1 and 10.1. NPM1-mutated vs KMT2A-rearranged: Wilcoxon p = 0.42 (HOXA9) and 0.40 (MEIS1); each vs other AML, p < 1e-8. Triangles: the two KMT2A-rearranged cases that also carry an NPM1 mutation. Boxes show median and interquartile range; whiskers reach 1.5 times the interquartile range.

## Figure 2. The NPM1 signature is robust to cell maturity and replicates in TCGA-LAML

**(A)** Shrunken log2FC for NPM1 status: the main design refitted on the 270 samples that have cell-type scores (x) against the sensitivity model that adds the HSC-like and monocyte-like scores of Bottomly et al. 2022 (y). Coloured: the 1,019 genes significant in the main model. Pearson r = 0.89; across the 1,019 genes the adjusted fold change is 0.85 times the main-design one (slope through zero), and 49 of the top 50 genes stay significant. HOXA9, PBX3 and CD34 keep 105%, 97% and 97% of their size. MEIS1 (117%) is partly circular, because it is one of the HSC-like signature genes. A model with all six scores is an upper bound only and is not shown (the six scores predict NPM1 status with AUC 0.93). Dashed line: identity.

**(B)** Shrunken log2FC in Beat AML (n = 276) against TCGA-LAML (n = 111: 31 NPM1-mutated, 80 wild-type; DESeq2 design ~ sex + NPM1 status), for the 17,498 genes tested in both cohorts, matched by Ensembl ID. Coloured: significant in Beat AML. Pearson r = 0.74 for all shared genes and 0.84 for the Beat AML significant genes; 95.4% of these go the same way in TCGA, and 48 of the 49 testable top-50 genes are significant again.

**(C)** Signature score in TCGA-LAML: mean z-score of the Beat AML top 50 up genes minus the top 50 down genes (47 and 49 present in TCGA). NPM1-mutated vs other AML: ROC AUC 0.992 (95% CI 0.981 to 1.000). KMT2A-rearranged cases (n = 9, not in the ROC) sit in between (median about 0.8, vs 1.5 in NPM1-mutated and -0.6 in other AML), consistent with a shared HOX half and an NPM1-specific down half of the signature.

**(D)** Predicting NPM1 status from expression with LASSO logistic regression. Thin solid lines: nested cross-validation in Beat AML (10 repeats of 5-fold outer CV, 10-fold inner CV choosing lambda.1se), one line per repeat. LASSO (final model 33 genes): CV AUC 0.985 (repeats 0.984 to 0.987); HOXA9 + CD34: 0.950 (0.948 to 0.952). Thick dashed lines: both models locked in Beat AML and tested once in TCGA-LAML after per-gene rescaling. LASSO AUC 0.996 (95% CI 0.989 to 1.000), 108 of 111 patients called correctly; HOXA9 + CD34 AUC 0.966 (0.934 to 0.997), 103 of 111; paired DeLong p = 0.046. Black: LASSO; grey: HOXA9 + CD34. Inset: false positive rate 0 to 0.25, sensitivity 0.75 to 1.

## Figure 3. A primitive-like NEK10/FTO/USP44-high subgroup inside NPM1-mutated AML

**(A)** The 100 NPM1-mutated Beat AML samples ordered by the core score (mean z-score of NEK10, FTO and USP44 VST). Dashed line: two-cluster k-means split, 63 low and 37 high (bimodality coefficient 0.73). Heat strip: z-score of each gene across the 100 samples. Tracks: subgroup; the Mer et al. 2021 primitive / committed call from 11 marker genes; FLT3-ITD (clinical assay); TET2, IDH2, DNMT3A, NRAS and PTPN11 from the exome (97 samples; white = no exome data). High vs low: TET2 33% vs 11% (padj 0.031), IDH2 39% vs 13% (padj 0.013), DNMT3A 25% vs 57% (padj 0.011), NRAS 0% vs 20% (padj 0.011), PTPN11 0% vs 25% (padj 0.008), FLT3-ITD 51% vs 35% (padj 0.20); Fisher's test, BH-adjusted across 10 features.

**(B)** Primitive score (mean z-score of the Mer et al. markers CDH2, GPR12, MDFI, ZNF521, PLXNB1 and HHIP) against the core score; Spearman 0.90. Shape: Mer et al. call. 32 of 37 high samples are primitive-like and 54 of 63 low samples committed-like (Fisher p = 5e-13). The markers were chosen in another cohort by another group; both scores come from the same RNA-seq.

**(C)** Subgroup DESeq2 (design ~ wave + specimen type + sex + group, NPM1 wild-type as reference): log2FC vs wild-type for NPM1-low (committed-like, n = 63; x axis) and NPM1-high (primitive-like, n = 37; y axis), coloured for the 1,019 main-model genes; grey, all other genes. "Mostly high" or "mostly low": one subgroup's fold change is at least twice the other's. 521 shared, 381 mostly high, 117 mostly low. The positive controls change in both subgroups (padj < 1e-4); NEK10, USP44 and the FTO locus rise in the high group only.

**(D)** FTO locus in TCGA-LAML (recount3 coverage, GRCh38, scaled to 40 million 100-base reads), one dot per patient: NPM1-mutated FTO-high (n = 6), FTO-low (n = 25) and NPM1 wild-type (n = 80). FTO exons 1 to 8 are not higher in the high group (slightly lower, p = 0.06). Exon 9, which FTO shares with IFEX9, rises about 12-fold; IFEX9 exon 1 coverage has a median of 256 vs 1.1; reads joining IFEX9 exon 1 to FTO exon 9 number 61 to 434 in all 6 high samples. IFEX9 starts in FTO intron 8 (Arza-Apalategi et al. 2023) and is listed in Ensembl 75 as FTO-007, so the Beat AML "FTO" count in panels A to C is the FTO locus / IFEX9, not the FTO enzyme. Pseudo-log y axis.

## Figure 4. Exploratory drug response and survival

**(A)** Ex vivo drug response in Beat AML (area under the dose-response curve after 3 days; lower = more sensitive), measured on the same specimen as RNA-seq, in 206 main-cohort samples with a monocyte-like score. Per drug, AUC ~ NPM1 status + monocyte-like score; BH correction across the 123 drugs with at least 20 tested samples per group (42 skipped). No drug reaches FDR < 0.05, and 90 of 123 have lower AUC in NPM1-mutated cells. Filled point: venetoclax (-16, FDR 0.35). Labelled: venetoclax and the six drugs with the smallest p-values.

**(B)** Within NPM1-mutated AML (analysis set 32 high, 48 low; the number tested varies by drug): AUC difference, primitive-like (high) minus committed-like (low), with 95% CI (AUC ~ group; report only). Top: the 15 drugs at FDR < 0.05 of 113 tested. Venetoclax -100 (median AUC 65 vs 204, FDR 8e-6); panobinostat and the MEK inhibitors selumetinib and trametinib are more active in the committed-like group, which carries all the NRAS and PTPN11 mutations. Bottom: the three drugs Mer et al. 2021 reported as more active in the primitive subtype do not come back clearly (sunitinib FDR 0.32, sorafenib 0.41, quizartinib 0.99).

**(C)** Overall survival (Kaplan-Meier, months from diagnosis; ticks mark censored patients) in the 275 main-cohort patients with known vital status (133 deaths), by NPM1 and FLT3-ITD status; numbers at risk below. The benefit sits in NPM1-mutated patients without FLT3-ITD: HR 0.41 (95% CI 0.25 to 0.67) vs everyone else, adjusted for age. NPM1 status alone is borderline (HR 0.70, p = 0.06, adjusted for age). Treatment varied and is not modelled.

**(D)** Overall survival within NPM1-mutated AML (n = 100, 44 deaths): NEK10/FTO/USP44-high (n = 37) vs low (n = 63). Cox HR 1.19 (95% CI 0.64 to 2.21), adjusted for age and FLT3-ITD; inconclusive, with 25% power to detect an HR of 1.5.
